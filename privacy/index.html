Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

PaulBooth.ai is a static archive site. It has no accounts, no shopping cart and no comment system. The only personal information it collects is what you choose to type into the contact form. To understand how the site is used, it also runs a cookieless visit counter and, where you allow it, a session-recording tool that captures how pages are scrolled and clicked, with the contact form hidden from it entirely.

The contact form

If you send an inquiry through the form on this site, you provide your name, your email address, an optional inquiry type and your message. That submission is passed to a small service run for this site, which formats it and emails it to Paul through Resend, an email delivery provider. Your email address is set as the reply address so Paul can answer you directly.

There is no database. Your message is not stored in any system beyond the email itself, which stays in Paul's mailbox for as long as it is useful to keep, and in Resend's delivery logs under their own retention policy. Your message is used to answer you and for nothing else. It is never sold, and it is not added to a mailing list.

To keep the form from being abused, the service briefly holds your IP address in memory to enforce a per-sender limit, and records it in its server logs if a submission is rejected as spam. Those counters expire within the hour and the logs are operational records, not a visitor profile. The service runs on Railway, which hosts it and retains those logs on its own schedule.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people actually look at. It is deliberately the least invasive option available: it sets no cookies, uses no local or session storage, does not fingerprint your device and does not follow you to any other website. What is reported back is aggregate — page views, referring sites, broad location such as country, browser and device type — and it is not tied to you as an individual.

Serving the measurement script means your browser contacts Cloudflare, and Cloudflare processes the request, including your IP address, to derive that coarse information. Cloudflare states that it does not use the IP address to build a profile or to track visitors across sites. Their handling is governed by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Session recordings and heatmaps

This site also uses Microsoft Clarity to see how the pages are actually used. Clarity records how you move through a page — scrolling, clicks, taps and mouse movement — and turns that into heatmaps and replayable session recordings, along with technical details such as your browser, device type, screen size and approximate location, which Microsoft derives from your IP address. These are used only to find what is confusing, broken or overlooked on the site.

The contact form is completely hidden from Clarity: nothing you type into it or select in it appears in any recording.

Clarity uses cookies so that it can tell a returning visitor from a new one, and so it only runs if you allow it — see the next section. Clarity data is processed and stored by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. This site runs Clarity for analytics only: its advertising features are switched off, and recordings are not combined with contact form submissions.

Cookies and your choices

Microsoft Clarity is the only thing on this site that sets cookies, and it is not needed for the site to work. YouTube may also set cookies, but only if you press play on a video — see the third parties below.

  • If you are in Europe (the EU and EEA, the UK and Switzerland, judged from your device's time zone), you are asked first, and Clarity does not load unless you accept.
  • Everywhere else, it loads by default, and you can turn it off at any time. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, that is treated as a refusal wherever you are.

If you decline, Clarity is not loaded at all. If you accept, Clarity sets _clck, which recognises your browser for up to a year, and _clsk, which links the pages of one visit and expires after a day. It also keeps a little data in session storage that is cleared when you close the tab, and Microsoft may set its own cookies on its own domains, such as clarity.ms and bing.com, which this site cannot remove.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, which is the one thing this site stores for itself. You can change it whenever you like: . Withdrawing consent removes Clarity's cookies from this site.

There is no advertising script and no tracking pixel.

What third parties may collect

Some parts of the site are delivered by other companies. When your browser loads that content, it necessarily contacts those companies directly, and they may record technical information such as your IP address, browser version and the page you were viewing. That processing is governed by their privacy policies, not this one.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub's servers log requests, including IP addresses, in order to serve pages and protect against abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare provides the analytics described above.
  • Microsoft provides Clarity, the session recording and heatmap tool described above.
  • Railway and Resend process and deliver contact form submissions, as described above. They are involved only if you send the form.
  • YouTube supplies the embedded videos. Embeds use the youtube-nocookie.com domain, which withholds personalised advertising cookies until you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube may set cookies and record playback. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to PaulBoothArt.com, PaulBoothBrand.com, DarkImages.com and to social profiles on Instagram and YouTube. Once you follow a link, the destination's own privacy policy applies.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and no information is knowingly collected from anyone under 18. Some artwork depicts mature and dark themes and is intended for an adult audience.

Your rights

The Cloudflare analytics are aggregate and hold nothing that identifies you. Clarity recordings are tied only to a random cookie ID, not to your name or email, and you can stop them at any time by changing your cookie choice above or by using a content or tracker blocker. If you have sent an inquiry through the contact form, you can ask for a copy of what was received, ask for a correction, or ask for the message to be deleted from Paul's mailbox. Write to the address below and the request will be answered. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
