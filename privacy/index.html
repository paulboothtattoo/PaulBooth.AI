Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

PaulBooth.ai is a static archive site. It has no accounts, no forms, no shopping cart and no comment system. It does not ask you for personal information, and it does not collect any directly.

What this site collects

Nothing. PaulBooth.ai sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and stores nothing in your browser's local or session storage. There is no tracking pixel and no visitor profiling.

What third parties may collect

Some parts of the site are delivered by other companies. When your browser loads that content, it necessarily contacts those companies directly, and they may record technical information such as your IP address, browser version and the page you were viewing. That processing is governed by their privacy policies, not this one.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub's servers log requests, including IP addresses, in order to serve pages and protect against abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • YouTube supplies the embedded videos. Embeds use the youtube-nocookie.com domain, which withholds personalised advertising cookies until you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube may set cookies and record playback. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Requests to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com expose your IP address to Google.

Links to other sites

This site links to PaulBoothArt.com, PaulBoothBrand.com, DarkImages.com and to social profiles on Instagram and YouTube. Once you follow a link, the destination's own privacy policy applies.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and no information is knowingly collected from anyone. Some artwork depicts mature and dark themes and is intended for an adult audience.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by this site, there is generally nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, or you want to ask how your information has been handled, get in touch and the request will be answered.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
